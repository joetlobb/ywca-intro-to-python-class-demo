Learning Objectives

By the end of this project, you'll be able to:

  • Combine variables, conditionals, loops, lists, and input() into one working program.
  • Debug your own code when something doesn't work as expected.
  • Show off a finished project you built yourself.

What You're Building

A simple multiple-choice quiz game: it asks a series of questions, checks each answer, and reports a score at the end. This project reuses everything from the earlier topics, so it's a good test of how well those pieces fit together — and something real you can show a friend, a case manager, or a potential employer.
